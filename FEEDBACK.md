| F5 | 2026-10-05 | Text / new shapes | Every new mind-map node wraps after about 15 characters, so almost every label ends up on two or three lines ("User have AFIN / and can login"). | Mind-map nodes grow in width to fit their text on one line, up to a max width, and only wrap past that. | F6 | 2026-10-05 | Layout / placeholder | Adding a placeholder ("Phase 0") pushes its shape down, so the tree connector into that shape bends instead of running straight. | The shape stays where it was, centred on the connector line. The placeholder sticks out above it, and the layout makes room above without moving the owner. | Cause: `layout` (`web/model.js` ~L281) adds the placeholder height to the node's span (`topSpace`), then centres the whole span (placeholder plus owner) on the parent, so `c.y = cross + (span - c.h + topSpace) / 2` lands the owner `topSpace / 2` below the parent's centre line. With a single child this is always visible as a bend. Fix: track each node's extent above and below its own centre line separately, centre children on the owners' centre lines, and count the placeholder only in the extent above. That keeps chains straight and still stops a placeholder from overlapping the sibling above. Check the vertical direction too, where `topSpace` is ignored today. |
